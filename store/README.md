# Google Play listing

Everything the Play Console's "Main store listing" asks for. The graphics are generated by
`android/tools/pixel/store.py`; the screenshots are the real app, photographed on an emulator by
the "Store screenshots" workflow and captioned by `store.py screenshots <folder>`.

| File | Play Console field | Spec |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `icon-512.png` | App icon | 512 x 512 PNG, no transparency; Play rounds the corners itself |
| `feature-graphic.png` | Feature graphic | 1024 x 500 PNG |
| `screenshots/*.png` | Phone screenshots | 1080 x 1920 PNG, upload in numbered order |

## Text

**App name** (30 characters max)

> DeBubble: Cozy Self-Care

**Short description** (80 characters max)

> Tiny daily self-care and brave little steps. A cozy pixel room grows with you.

**Full description** (4000 characters max)

> DeBubble is a cozy pixel-art room that grows as you look after yourself and step a little further out of your bubble.
>
> Every day there are four tiny things to do: one for your body, one for your room, one for your heart, and one small step outside. Each takes a few minutes. Do them and your room gets brighter, tidier and fuller. Skip a few days and it gets a little messy, but nothing you earned is ever taken away.
>
> FOUR TINY THINGS A DAY
> • Body, room and heart routines you pick yourself, tied to a moment you already have ("when I put the kettle on")
> • One mission outside, sized to where you are today: a new street, a new café, a hello
> • Too much today? Say what got in the way and it becomes a spark of courage, not a failure
>
> A ROOM THAT GROWS
> • Start with an empty room and earn every lamp, plant and poster
> • The more you go outside, the more the room opens up: a window, then a door, then the street
> • Dress your little you, and earn hats as you level up
>
> GENTLE, NOT NAGGING
> • "Remind me" sits right next to "I did it": pick a time and get one nudge, or none at all
> • A home-screen widget shows your room and today's four
> • No streaks to lose. Days you showed up only ever go up
>
> BUILT ON HOW HABITS AND COURAGE ACTUALLY WORK
> • Tiny habits tied to cues, so they stick
> • Graded steps outside your comfort zone, one rung at a time
> • Guess how a mission will go before you try it, then see how it really went. It is usually easier than you thought
> • Journeys: 30-stop paths toward something you want, like a real circle of friends or a life worth describing
>
> PRIVATE BY DESIGN
> No account, no ads, no tracking. DeBubble does not even have internet access: everything you write stays on your phone.
>
> DeBubble is a self-help tool, not a substitute for professional care. If you are struggling, please reach out to someone you trust or a local support service.
